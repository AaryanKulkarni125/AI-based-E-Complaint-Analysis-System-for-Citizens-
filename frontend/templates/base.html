Citizen Grievance Redressal — Help & FAQ

1. How to File a Complaint?

Log in as a Citizen, click Submit Complaint, specify the ward, enter the location (or use "Auto-Detect Location"), describe the issue in at least 20 characters, and attach photos (JPEG/PNG/PDF up to 5MB). The AI engine automatically classifies the category and urgency.

2. How Does Tracking Work?

Every grievance receives a unique tracking ID format (e.g. CMP-000001). You can check real-time status under My Grievances.

3. Resolution Confirmation & Reopening

Once an officer marks your grievance as Resolved with proof remarks, you can submit a 1 to 5 star rating. If unsatisfied, you can reopen the complaint within 7 days.

4. Municipal Helpline & Contacts

Toll-Free Civic Helpline: 1800-111-2026 (24x7) | Email: support@municipal.gov.in
